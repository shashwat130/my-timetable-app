Class tests and syllabus

Add each class test with its date and the lectures it covers. The planner moves those lectures to the front of the queue in order of test date, aims to finish them one day before the test, marks the test and revision day on the calendar, and re-plans everything else around them.

Each day box lists the work for that day: Ph = Pharmacology, Pa = Pathology, Mi = Microbiology with lecture numbers, then Biz with the business tasks, plus any test or your own tasks. A faded line means finished. Tap a day for the full list.

The whole 56-week plan, on your calendar. Each roadmap week is a block of hours. Your free blocks are 2 hours (5:00 to 7:00 am, Monday to Saturday) and two 3-hour blocks on Sunday, about 18 hours a calendar week. Tick each session on the day you do it. A skipped day pushes every later week back, so the dates always show where you really are.
The roadmap assumes full-time (about 40 h). At 36 h, each roadmap week takes about two of your calendar weeks.
